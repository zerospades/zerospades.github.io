What is ZeroSpades

The game you remember,
the experience you deserve.

ZeroSpades is a fork of OpenSpades, the open-source reimplementation of Ace of Spades 0.75. It builds on the original with a completely reworked HUD, new animations, mod support, physics improvements, and a long list of quality-of-life additions — all while staying fully compatible with the existing server ecosystem.

Compatible with vanilla, piqueserver, PySnip, pyspades, and SpadeX servers. Free, open-source, licensed under the GPLv3.

What's different

Built on OpenSpades.
Taken further.

Reworked HUD & UI

Fully redesigned interface: health bar with damage animation, hotbar for tools, configurable crosshair and scope, HUD color and safe zone settings, floating damage numbers, a client-side hit analyzer showing player name, distance, and body part, and an extended block color palette.

Animations & Models

Improved firstperson weapon animations, more thirdperson animations, firstperson playermodels, dynamic playermodels by weapon class, classic firstperson viewmodel, left-handed viewmodel, and configurable viewmodel position presets.

Mod System

Load weapon skins, sound packs, fonts, and UI paks from the built-in Mods screen. One click to download official mods, enable, reorder, or disable — no file hunting required.

Demo Recording

Record and replay gameplay demos directly in ZeroSpades. Record from the command line and play them back in-game — useful for reviewing matches or capturing footage.

Physics & Effects

Ragdoll corpse physics with configurable removal, falling block physics, physically-based lighting for models, smooth player orientation interpolation, enhanced ragdoll physics, and the same classic fog tint as the original client.

Stats & Killfeed

Track hit accuracy, K/D ratio, kill streaks, melee and grenade kills, and blocks placed. Killfeed icons with kill indicators (non-scoped, in-air), domination tracking, killstreak sounds, and player names visible in spectator mode.

Customization

Make it yours
with mods

ZeroSpades ships with a built-in mod manager. Browse and install paks directly from the game — weapon skins, sound packs, UI fonts, and more from the community-maintained zerospades-paks repository.

  • Weapon model replacements — AK-47, M4, MP5K, USP, Remington, and more
  • Sound packs including custom killstreak sounds
  • Custom fonts for the game interface
  • Weapon charms and keychains
